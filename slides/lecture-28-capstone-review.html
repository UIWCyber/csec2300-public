Domain 1.0 - General Security Concepts (12%)

  • CIA, AAA, and non-repudiation (L1); control categories and functions (L2); zero trust; crypto and PKI (L12/L13); IAM concepts (L14).
  • "You signed commits and ran TLS. That is this domain."
TipThis domain is vocabulary-heavy and high-yield for its weight. The CIA and AAA distinctions are guaranteed questions.

Domain 2.0 - Threats, Vulnerabilities & Mitigations (22%)

  • Threat actors (L2); supply chain (L8/L19); vulnerability scanning (L7/L10); prompt injection and the OWASP LLM Top 10 (L21); social engineering.
  • The highest-weight domain after Operations, and the AI-security material lives here.
NoteThe injection and agent work from M8/M9 is not a side topic. It is squarely in the second-heaviest exam domain.

Domain 4.0 - Security Operations (28%)

  • CI/CD and automation (L7); hardening and baselines (L10); logging, monitoring, and audit trails (L6/L22); IAM operations (L14); incident response (L25).
  • The biggest slice of the exam. Most questions live here, and your labs were operations practice.
TipHalf your study time belongs here and in Domain 2.0; together they are half the exam.

Where to Focus

  • Self-rate each domain 1 to 5; study the lowest first. Operations (4.0) and Threats (2.0) are half the exam combined.
  • Reuse everything: all quizzes, the midterm, and lab READMEs are blueprinted to these domains. Re-work missed items.
  • The final review guide posts today; the final exam during finals week is comprehensive and blueprinted to SY0-701.
NoteRetrieval beats re-teaching. Re-working a missed quiz question sticks better than re-reading the slide.

In-Class Activity

Domain self-diagnosis + study plan.

  1. 1Students rate themselves 1 to 5 on each of the five domains on the review guide.
  2. 2Pick the two lowest and list the specific lectures and labs to revisit (the mapping is on Slides 6 through 10).
  3. 3Write one concrete study action per weak domain (for example, "re-work Quiz 4 and the container-hardening lab").
  4. 4Share one action with a neighbor for accountability. This becomes your personalized final-exam prep plan.
